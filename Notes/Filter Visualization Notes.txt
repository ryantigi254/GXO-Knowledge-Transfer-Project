Filter and Visualisation Method Notes

1. Profile feature distributions to understand scale and skew.
2. Inspect categorical coverage to identify dominant classes.
3. Check numeric correlations to detect redundant or interacting fields.
4. Compare candidate rules with visual diagnostics before operational use.
5. Prefer interpretable charts that support decision review by stakeholders.

Operational guidance
- Keep filtering logic explicit and auditable.
- Validate each rule against edge cases before adoption.
