Geo-Consolidation Method Notes

1. Convert spatial references into comparable latitude/longitude coordinates.
2. Compute distance between candidate delivery points (for example, haversine for straight-line screening).
3. Apply business-rule filters before proposing consolidation candidates.
4. Rank candidate pairs by operational value and feasibility constraints.
5. Validate candidates with an operator feedback loop and maintain a reviewed rule set.

Improvement directions
- Replace brute-force pair checks with spatial indexing/clustering.
- Add road-network or travel-time constraints.
- Capture operator approvals/rejections as explicit model feedback.
