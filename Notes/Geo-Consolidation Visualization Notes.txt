Geo-Consolidation Visualisation Notes

Recommended visual set
1. Scatter map of delivery points for geographic coverage.
2. Candidate-link overlay to inspect plausible consolidation routes.
3. Distance distribution plot to tune radius/travel thresholds.
4. Ranked opportunity view for prioritisation.

Usage guidance
- Use visuals to support review, not to replace feasibility checks.
- Keep map outputs out of public repos when built from private data.
